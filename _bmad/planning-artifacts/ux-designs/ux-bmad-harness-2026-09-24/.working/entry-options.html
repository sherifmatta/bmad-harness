Story 7.5: where the Skill Session entry lives

Two placements of the same entry, everything else held equal. In both, the empty chat is open (the entry is the current row), and two skill sessions are already live: bmad-build working, bmad-create-epics-and-stories waiting for a message. Chat copy is deliberately left empty: it is decided after the placement. Grey discs stand in for the illustrated portraits. Dark theme only.

Skill disc shown as a neutral initials disc holding / in mono (a proposal, decided separately). Skill seats are drawn shorter than persona tiles (44px, no portrait) so they do not read as a sixth persona STAND-IN

For A

  • All the chats live in one list. The door and the seats it makes sit next to each other, which is what you first reached for.
  • Reads as one more kind of conversation beside the five personas and the room, which is what the epic says it is.
  • The Sprint board row stays the only navigation row; the top of the sidebar does not grow.

Against A

  • It sits at the end of the persona list. With more than about six personas the list scrolls and the entry goes below the fold.
  • A tile-shaped row is the closest the sidebar gets to saying "a participant", the one thing a skill session must not be (FR134). The slash disc and "no persona" have to do that work alone.
  • The persona list is no longer only personas, which weakens the grouping rule (module and team, config order).

For B

  • Always in the same place at the top, however many personas the install has. It never scrolls away.
  • Looks like a way in, not a member of the team: a plain row with no disc, beside the other row that is a place you go. That fits "an entry to a chat, not a launcher".
  • The persona list stays only personas, in config order.
  • The nav row already has a "current" look, which gives the reuse rule a natural reading: the row stays lit while the empty chat is open and a second click lands on it.

Against B

  • The door and its seats are apart: the entry at the top, the seats in their own group at the bottom. Your instinct to keep the chats together is only half met.
  • Sprint board is a read-only view; Skill Session opens a chat. Two rows that look alike do different kinds of thing.
  • The top of the sidebar grows by one row (about 30px), pushing every persona tile down.
  • The seats still need a group heading, which A does not.
