3 — The untested requirement: agents nobody curated

AD-16: "every other installed agent derives params from its agent id" and "design must hold up for arbitrary unknown agents, not just the five." The mockup proves only the curated case. These are generated from the agent id alone — same id, same face, on any machine, forever.

What to look for. Not whether any single one is handsome — whether all sixteen read as the same company. A palette that drifts, a hair shape that reads as a hat, or one face that looks like a different product is the failure mode. The curated five are tuned; these are not, and the product ships whatever this function returns.

4 — The 20px test

The hardest requirement in AD-16 is "legible from 20px". At this size the eyes are roughly one pixel. Curated and derived, mixed, in the order the stacked-faces component would render them.

The honest question. At 20px these are colour blobs with a hair silhouette. That may be fine — a stacked face is an identity token, not a portrait, and distinguishability matters more than detail. But if two agents land on similar hair and skin, they become the same blob, and the derivation has no collision check.

5 — Determinism

AD-16 requires the same agent to wear the same face across sessions and machines, asserted in tests. Each id rendered three times from three separate calls.

What I need you to judge

Q1Does section 1 read as a plausible colleague, or as a toy? This is the whole risk. If your gut says "cartoon", the direction changes now and not after it's built.
Q2Does section 3 hold together as one company? Sixteen derived faces, no hand-tuning. If they drift, the derivation needs tighter palettes — or the five stay curated and everyone else gets something simpler and honest.
Q3Is 20px carrying its weight, or should stacked faces be initials instead? The product already has an .initials component for the user. Using it for tiny agent references is a legitimate option AD-16 doesn't forbid.
Q4Is the neutral expression right? Every face has the same faint smile and level brows. Reactive faces are deferred to the Should tier, so this one expression is what ships — it has to survive being the only one.
