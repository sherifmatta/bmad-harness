Option A — the memlog becomes the body

my lean

The card never has an empty body; it has two modes and both are content. When prose isn't streaming, the body is the reasoning log in its own register — tabular time, a kind tag, the decision and the reason it was made.

What it buys. It delivers "you watch the why instead of being told it" for free, and the driest box in the ADR does become the most interesting thing on screen. What it costs. doc:prose and run:note are separate channels precisely so they never mix — AD-3 routes them apart on purpose. Putting them in one box asks the register alone to keep them distinct.

Option B — a separate reasoning band

safest

A distinct strip sits under the head and carries the reasons. The prose body stays where it is — empty at the start of a run, or holding its last state mid-run. Two channels, two boxes, no ambiguity ever.

What it buys. The two feeds can never be confused, and the band can persist during streaming so reasons keep arriving alongside prose. What it costs. That empty-body line is doing a spinner's job in words — the one thing the constraint exists to prevent. And the card now has four stacked regions before any prose appears.

Option C — foot-anchored

weakest

Reasons accumulate in the footer beside the not-yet-written sections, treated as run metadata rather than content. The card stays a single object with nothing added to it.

What it buys. Nothing is added to the card's anatomy; the head/body/foot shape survives untouched. What it costs. The footer is 10.5px --faint metadata — the quietest tier in the product. Putting the single most interesting content of the run in the least legible slot inverts the hierarchy, and the empty body problem is still there.
