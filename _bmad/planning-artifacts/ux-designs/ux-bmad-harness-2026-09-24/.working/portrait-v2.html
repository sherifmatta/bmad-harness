Portrait v2

v1 read as a toy. This is the revision, side by side with the original at every size the product renders. The 20px case is gone — it's initials now — which buys the face a much larger detail budget.

v1 · mockup:825v2 · this revisionstill pure · no ids, no gradients, no assets

1 — What changed, and why

face shape
A symmetrical ellipse became a path with a tapered jaw and chin. Width-to-height went from .89 to .76 — the single biggest cause of the cartoon read.
light source
Everything was flat fill. Now there is one light, upper-left: a shadow down the right contour, a cheek highlight, a shadow under the jaw falling on the neck, and an inner-ear shadow.
eyes
Round white ellipses with a big pupil became almond paths with a distinct iris, a smaller pupil, a catchlight and a weighted upper lid.
brows
Thin uniform strokes became filled tapered shapes, thicker at the inner end.
nose
A single squiggle became a bridge shadow, a tip shadow and two nostrils.
mouth
A symmetrical smile arc became a flatter line with a lower-lip highlight. Less smiling, more resting.
hair
One flat fill became a mass plus a highlight arc, so it reads as hair rather than a helmet.

4 — Small references are initials now

Where the portrait used to render at 20px it renders nothing. Initials on the mockup's existing token, hover or focus for the name and role. Keyboard reachable, so it works without a mouse.

hover any face

Why this is better than a 20px portrait. Initials are unambiguous at any size, they never collide the way two hashed faces can, and they carry the name directly rather than asking you to recognise a blob. The portrait keeps every size where it earns its place — 32 and up.
