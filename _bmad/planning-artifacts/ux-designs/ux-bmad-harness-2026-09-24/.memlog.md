- (decision) PORTRAIT ART DIRECTION — the mockup's current generator (harness-mockup.html:825) READS AS A TOY and is rejected as-is. - (decision) The 20px stacked-face case DROPS THE PORTRAIT and uses initials instead, with a hover revealing the persona name and role. The product already ships an .initials component (32px circle, #2b333d bg, #39424e border, mono 11px #aab4c1) for the user; it is reused for small agent references. CONSEQUENCE: AD-16's stated 'legible from 20px to tile size' relaxes to 'legible from 32px', which gives the portrait a larger detail budget at every size it does render.
