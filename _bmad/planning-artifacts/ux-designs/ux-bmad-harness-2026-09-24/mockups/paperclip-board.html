1Overview cardsPaperclip's dashboard opens on four cards: agents enabled, tasks in progress, spend against budget, pending approvals. Same four here. Stories and approvals come from sprint-status.yaml and the open gates; the harness already reads both.
2Spend cardPaperclip tracks cost per agent against a monthly budget and stops agents at the limit. Here it is read-only: run cost per persona and per epic, no budget enforcement. Needs a cost source from the runs, which I have not checked exists.
3Approvals card is the only highlighted oneOne thing asks for you; the rest are informational. Clicking it opens the Inbox.
4Board by statusPaperclip's task breakdown counts by status. Here the BMad statuses are real columns: backlog, ready for dev, in progress, review, done. Cards show the owning persona and a live bar on a running story.Drag to move is left out on purpose: status changes come from the skills, not from the board.
5Agents panel with what each is doingPaperclip's agent cards show a state and the current work. Idle personas dim, as in its org chart and lists.
6Activity feedA company-wide feed of status changes, comments, approvals and agent state changes, as on Paperclip's dashboard. Here it lists persona events across rooms; it can be reconstructed from the run notes.
