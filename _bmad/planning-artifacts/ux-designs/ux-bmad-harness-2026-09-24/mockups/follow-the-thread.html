The thread follows the conversation

The same behaviour in a persona's thread and in the party room. While you are at the bottom it keeps the newest line in view; scroll up and it stops moving; one quiet line brings you back. Drawn in the shipped dark tokens; the control has no theme-specific value.

1 · following
At the bottom. New text, a new turn, a card or a decision block keeps the bottom edge in view in the same frame. No smooth scroll, no easing. No control is shown, because there is nowhere else to go.
2 · paused
Scrolled up to reread. Nothing moves the page under you, even while the persona keeps writing. ↓ latest appears at the bottom edge. It has no count, no “new”, no dot.
3 · party room, paused
Same in the room. One speaker's words stream while you reread an earlier exchange. The control, the rule and the way back are identical.
