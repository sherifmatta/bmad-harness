MaryBusiness Analyst14:35 evidence

Both of you are arguing cost. Nobody has said what changes for the merchant, so let me put the evidence in: of 214 payout tickets last quarter, 61% are "where is my money", not "my money is wrong". That's an observability complaint.

Which means — and John, this is aimed at you — the ledger's real justification is the event history you can show a merchant, not the engineering hygiene. If we're not shipping that history in v1, we're paying for a ledger we don't use.

SallyUX Designer14:36 disagrees · framing
The question as John framed it

I want to push on the framing itself. You've all called this an internal decision. It isn't. A merchant sees four states today — pending, sent, failed, unknown — and "unknown" is the one they call support about. The architecture picks how many honest states I'm able to draw.
