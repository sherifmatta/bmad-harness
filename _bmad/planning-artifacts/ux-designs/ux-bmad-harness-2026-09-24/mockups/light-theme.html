What it is. A cream ground (#f5f2ec, panels #fffdf9) with warm greys and the same blue accent. Softer and more like a page than a tool.
Faces, and the cost. Backdrops are lifted to pastels so the faces sit in the page. It is the gentler look, but the ground now carries warmth too, which spends the budget the faces are meant to have to themselves, and the pale backdrops lower the contrast of lighter-haired faces (John). It also means a portrait's backdrop becomes theme-dependent, which today's closed parameter table does not allow.

Contrast, measured

WCAG ratios against the surface each text sits on · the locked dark ramp for comparison

Reading it. Every pair that carries text clears 4.5 except meta. The dark ramp's faint tier (3.6) is lifted to 4.6 in A and 5.2 in B, so the "no actionable fact at faint" rule is easier to keep in light. meta stays low in both (3.4, 3.7, as in dark at 3.1): it holds only timestamps and micro-labels. Several accents and state colours sit lower than their dark twins (accent 5.8 vs 7.0, amber 5.0 vs 7.2) because they were darkened to hold ≥5 on white; none falls below it.
State colours. Green, amber, red and note stay four, mean the same things, and are driven by the same facts. Their halos are strengthened slightly (.14 → .16) because a glow reads weaker on a light ground.

Where the switch lives

independent of which palette you pick

My recommendation. 2, plus 3 as a courtesy. It keeps the topbar as it is, and "follow the system" is the right default for a desktop tool.
What gets stored. One value (system | light | dark) in harness bookkeeping under userData, never in a project.

What this changes in the spec

  • NFR15 (dark only) is reversed. It also bans a prefers-color-scheme branch and a high-contrast variant. A light theme needs the first two lifted and the third left banned.
  • tokens.css keeps its verbatim dark block untouched. The light values arrive as a second :root[data-theme="light"] block, so the byte-for-byte test on the first block still holds. Main sets the attribute from the stored choice and the OS appearance; no component reads a media query.
  • About seventy colour literals in DESIGN.md need a light twin each (the mapping used here is the starting table). The tokens test needs to cover both blocks.
  • Portraits. Palette A needs no portrait change. Palette B makes bg theme-dependent and reopens the closed parameter table (FR77).
  • Document window, scrim and shadows flip too: the window's shadow becomes a cool low-alpha one, and the scrim lightens.
